03 · REPLICATION VS VARIATION

Know which workflow you need

AreaReplicationContent variation
GoalRebuild one reference videoCreate a related series
CutsPreserve source cut pointsPreserve shot grammar, not every cut
ActionsMinimal adaptationDesign a new core action chain
Valid changeModel, product, sceneHook, format, benefit, action, shots, scenario
Not true variation:

Changing only the background, color, accessory, or one sentence. Do not force OOTD, GRWM, product demonstration, and every other format into one video.

First provide a five-version strategy matrix. Each version must have one primary format and one core benefit. At least two of these must change: opening hook, core action chain, shot combination, or scenario. Background, color, copy, or accessory changes alone do not count.

Produce version 1 only: theme, complete timeline from 0.00 seconds, exactly 16 continuous panels, and PASS/FIX/UNKNOWN quality review. Wait for approval before producing the other versions or final platform prompts.
